We build a quantitative HANK model with structural monopsony and multi-bloc
input-output networks, solved via sequence-space Jacobians. Three results
emerge. First, countercyclical wage markdowns endogenously flatten the
Phillips curve (slope falls 1.4\% in baseline). Second, geoeconomic
fragmentation concentrates Domar weights in high-monopsony upstream sectors,
amplifying wage suppression non-linearly; a 15pp fragmentation shock reduces
TFP by 0.47\%. Third, the welfare-optimal Taylor rule accommodates supply-side
inflation ($\phi_\phi^* = -0.22$), not fights it. A commuting-zone
Bartik design confirms the IO$\times$Monopsony wage interaction in 2021--2023
microdata.
